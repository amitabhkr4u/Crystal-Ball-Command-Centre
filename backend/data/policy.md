# Approvals Policy Note (v1)

## 1. Review order

Review pending approvals in this order: safety and sensor documents first, because they gate field use; onboarding folders and patrol checklists second; demonstration videos third; reference images and layout maps last, unless they change a safety zone. When two items sit at the same level, review the one that has waited longest.

## 2. What to check by file type

Folder: confirm every checklist is present, current, and opens correctly. Video: watch the full demo, confirm it shows the approved patrol procedure, and check that no restricted area is visible. PDF: confirm it is the latest version, that sensor and equipment specs are complete, and that page numbers and sign-offs are present. Image: confirm that zone labels and camera positions are legible and match the current site layout.

## 3. Approve, reject or request changes

Approve only when every check passes. Request changes when the content is correct but incomplete, such as a missing page or an unclear label. Reject when the content is wrong, outdated, or unsafe, and always write a reason of at least one sentence so the submitter can fix it. Never approve an item you have not opened.

## 4. Escalation

Escalate to the Safety Lead within one working day if an item describes safety equipment you cannot verify, shows a restricted area, or conflicts with an earlier approved document. Do not approve or reject an escalated item until the Safety Lead replies.

## 5. Turnaround and records

Decide within two working days of submission. Every decision is logged with the reviewer name, the date, and the reason. Approved items become visible to patrol operators immediately; rejected items return to the submitter with your reason.
